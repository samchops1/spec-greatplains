Get to know us

Looking for a quality lawn and landscape service?

Since 1995 Great Plains Lawn Care LLC has been committed to serving clients in the Kansas City area with their lawn and landscape needs. We offer a variety of services to keep your outdoor living space looking its best.

We provide courteous and reliable service. We’d love the opportunity to work with you and show why our personalized service sets us apart from the rest of the field.

We pride ourselves on being receptive to the unique needs of each client. Our dedicated staff invests the time and energy to be well prepared for unique requests or special concerns.

Our services

The work we do

From regularly scheduled mowing to summer hardscape projects and a trailer that hauls what will fit — this is the list from Paul’s shop, in his words.

Mowing and fertilizing

Are you interested in working with a professional lawn service? We have the equipment and skills to keep your lawn in top shape with regularly scheduled mowing, fertilization, aeration, verticutting and seeding.

  • Mowing
  • Fertilization
  • Aeration
  • Verticutting
  • Seeding

Landscaping and pruning

Get the results you want for a reasonable price. Our hardworking team will get the job done right. We can add or replace plants, mulch, plant annuals in late spring or early fall, prune trees or trim bushes.

  • Planting
  • Mulch
  • Annuals
  • Pruning
  • Bush trim

Decks, paths, fences & arbors

We want you to enjoy your outdoor space. Our summer schedule includes decks, fences, arbors, raised bed gardens, sheds, stone paths, fence staining or painting, and rock retaining walls. Services are done quickly and efficiently. We understand how important it is to feel confident and worry-free when it comes to outdoor projects.

  • Decks
  • Fences
  • Arbors
  • Stone paths
  • Raised beds
  • Sheds
  • Retaining walls

Spring and fall clean up

Our crew can clean up leaves, brush and overwintered annuals. We specialize in leaf clean up with our backpack blowers and giant leaf vac. Let us get your yard and landscape ready for each season.

  • Leaves
  • Brush
  • Overwintered annuals
  • Leaf vac

Trash hauling

Get this service done quickly and efficiently by the experts here at Great Plains Lawn Care LLC. We can clean up storm damage, construction materials, basements, and garages. If it can fit on a trailer we can haul it away. Give us a call for a free estimate.

  • Storm damage
  • Construction materials
  • Basements
  • Garages
